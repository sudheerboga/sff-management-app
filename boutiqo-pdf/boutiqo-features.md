# Boutiqo — App Overview & Feature Descriptions

---

## About Boutiqo

Boutiqo is a complete boutique management app built specifically for Indian tailors and fashion boutique owners. Running a boutique means juggling hundreds of orders, remembering every customer's measurements, tracking payments, managing staff — all while delivering perfect garments on time.

Boutiqo replaces the paper registers, scattered notebooks, and missed deadlines with one elegant, easy-to-use app. From the moment a customer places an order to the day they collect their garment, every detail is captured, organised, and accessible in seconds — right from your phone.

No technical knowledge needed. No complicated setup. Just open the app and start managing your boutique the modern way.

---

## Who is Boutiqo for?

Boutiqo is designed for **boutique owners and their staff** who handle custom stitching orders — blouses, churidars, lehengas, tops, and all kinds of traditional and contemporary garments. Whether you run a small home boutique or a busy multi-staff shop, Boutiqo scales with you.

## Key Features

---

### 1. Smart Order Management

Create and manage every customer order in one place. Boutiqo keeps track of exactly what was ordered, for whom, at what price, and when it needs to be delivered — so nothing ever slips through the cracks.

- Create orders with item-wise amounts (e.g. Blouse ₹1,200 + Churidar ₹800)
- Set order dates and delivery dates for each order
- Update order status — **In Progress**, **Ready**, **Delivered**
- Search orders instantly by customer name or phone number
- Apply filters to find orders by status, date, or amount
- Attach reference photos (fabric images, design references) directly to each order item
- All orders are linked to the customer's profile and their measurements

---

### 2. Complete Customer Profiles with Family Members

Your customers are more than just a phone number. Boutiqo stores a full profile for each customer, including every family member they bring for stitching — their child, spouse, mother, or anyone else.

- One account per customer, multiple family members underneath
- Each family member is tracked separately (Self, Child, Spouse, etc.)
- See the complete order history for every customer at a glance
- Measurements are saved per family member, not just the account holder
- Search customers instantly by name, phone, or member name
- View total orders placed and last order date directly from the customer list

---

### 3. Digital Measurements — No More Paper Registers

Boutiqo replaces your measurement book with a clean, digital measurement system. Store precise measurements for every garment type, for every customer and their family members. Access them instantly when creating a new order — no flipping through pages.

- Custom garment templates — create templates for Blouse, Churidar, Lehenga, Pant, Top, or any garment you stitch
- Add unlimited custom fields to each garment template (e.g. Shoulder, Chest, Waist, Hip, Length, Sleeve Length, Neck Depth, and more)
- Measurements are organised per family member — each member has their own set of saved measurements
- Measurements are automatically linked to new orders when the same customer places one
- Copy measurements from one family member to another in seconds
- Share a customer's measurements directly via WhatsApp — useful when another tailor or branch needs them

---

### 4. Payment & Profit Tracking

Know exactly where every rupee goes. Boutiqo tracks every payment a customer makes — advances, part payments, and final payments — and shows you the net profit on every order after material costs.

- Record advance payments and part instalments with the exact date
- Add material cost separately from the stitching amount
- Boutiqo auto-calculates the **net profit** per order (Total Bill − Material Cost)
- Visual payment progress bar shows how much of the total has been collected (e.g. 81% paid)
- Full payment history for each order — who collected it, when, and how much
- See "Fully Paid" status at a glance on every order

---

### 5. WhatsApp Bill Sharing

Send a professional bill to your customer directly through WhatsApp — without typing anything manually. One tap from the order screen generates a clean bill and opens WhatsApp to send it straight to the customer.

- Generate a bill for any order with a single tap
- Bill is sent via WhatsApp — no need for a separate billing app
- Measurements can also be shared via WhatsApp directly from the measurement screen
- Customers receive a clean, professional summary they can save and refer to

---

### 6. Staff Management

Invite your tailors, assistants, and co-workers to use Boutiqo with their own phone number. Each staff member gets access only to what they need — your business data stays secure and organised.

- Invite staff by entering their name and phone number
- Staff log in with their own phone OTP — no passwords to remember
- Two role types: **Admin** (full access) and **Staff** (day-to-day operations)
- Activate or deactivate any staff member at any time
- Every action (payments, orders) is tracked and attributed to the staff member who performed it
- Each boutique's data is completely isolated — staff from one boutique cannot see another boutique's data

---

### 7. Business Reports

Understand your boutique's performance with clear, visual reports. See how much revenue you made this month, which customers order the most, and how your orders are progressing.

- Monthly revenue table — see income for each month at a glance
- Bar and line charts for revenue trends over time
- Order status donut chart — how many are In Progress vs. Delivered
- Top customers report — who contributes the most to your business
- Reports update automatically as you add new orders and payments

---

### 8. Subscription Plans

Boutiqo offers flexible plans so boutiques of all sizes can get started and grow without worrying about cost. Start free and upgrade only when you need more.

- **Free Plan** — up to 100 orders and 2 staff members, with all core features included
- **Professional Plan** — for growing boutiques that need more orders and a larger team
- **Premium Plan** — for large boutiques and chains with custom requirements
- Subscription status and validity dates are visible from inside the app
- Upgrade directly through the Subscription screen

## How to Install

Boutiqo is a **Progressive Web App (PWA)** — it installs directly from your browser, just like a native app. No App Store. No Play Store. No waiting for approval.

**On iPhone (Safari only):**
1. Open the Boutiqo link in **Safari**
2. Tap the **Share** button at the bottom
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**
4. Tap **Add** — the app appears on your home screen

**On Android (Chrome):**
1. Open the Boutiqo link in **Chrome**
2. Tap the **menu ( ⋮ )** button
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen → Install**
4. The app icon appears on your home screen

Once installed, Boutiqo opens fullscreen — indistinguishable from a native app.
