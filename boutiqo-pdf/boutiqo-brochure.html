  • Multiple family members per account (Self, Child, Spouse)

  • Full order history per customer at a glance
  • Measurements linked per family member
  • Quick new order creation from customer profile

Digital Precision

Perfect Measurements,
Every Time

Replace your paper register with a clean digital system. Store precise measurements per garment, per family member, forever.

  • Custom garment templates — Blouse, Churidar, Lehenga, Pant and more
  • Unlimited custom fields per garment type
  • Measurements auto-linked when placing a new order
  • Copy measurements across family members instantly
  • Share measurements via WhatsApp with one tap

Financial Clarity

Payments &
Profit Tracking

Know exactly where every rupee goes. Track advances, instalments, material costs — and see your profit on every single order.

  • Record advance and instalment payments with dates
  • Track material cost separately from the total bill
  • Auto-calculated net profit per order
  • Visual payment progress bar (% collected)
  • Full payment history with staff attribution
